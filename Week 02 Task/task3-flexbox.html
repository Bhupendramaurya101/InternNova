About

Kamla Nehru Institute of Technology is a state government, autonomous engineering institution located in Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. It has been ranked amongst the top engineering institutes under AKTU. College Founded: 1976.


Academic Programs

The institute offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes in various academic fields.

B.Tech

The B.Tech programme is an undergraduate programme with a duration of 4 years and 8 semesters.

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is a postgraduate programme with a duration of 2 years and 4 semesters.

M.Tech

The institute offers full-time and part-time postgraduate M.Tech programmes. The part-time programme consists of five semesters.

The institute offers various academic programmes through different departments and academic disciplines.

Applied Sciences & Humanities

The Department of Applied Sciences & Humanities provides foundational education in mathematics, physics, chemistry, and humanities.

Civil Engineering

The Department of Civil Engineering focuses on construction, infrastructure, structural engineering, and related engineering fields.

Electrical Engineering

The Department of Electrical Engineering focuses on electrical systems, power generation, circuits, and electrical technologies.

Electronics Engineering

The Department of Electronics Engineering focuses on electronic systems, communication, circuits, and related technologies.

Mechanical Engineering

The Department of Mechanical Engineering focuses on machines, manufacturing, mechanical systems, and industrial technologies.

Computer Science & Engineering

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering focuses on computer science, software development, information technology, and related computing fields.
