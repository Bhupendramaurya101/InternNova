About

Kamla Nehru Institute of Technology is a state government, autonomous engineering institution located in Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. It has been ranked amongst the top engineering institutes under AKTU. The institute was founded in 1976.

Academic Programs

The institute offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes in various academic fields.

B.Tech

The B.Tech programme is an undergraduate programme with a duration of 4 years and 8 semesters.

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is a postgraduate programme with a duration of 2 years and 4 semesters.

M.Tech

The institute offers full-time and part-time postgraduate M.Tech programmes. The part-time programme consists of five semesters.

Doctoral Programs

The institute offers doctoral programs in various engineering and applied science disciplines, providing opportunities for advanced research and academic development.

Departments

The institute offers various academic programmes through different departments and academic disciplines.

Applied Sciences & Humanities

The Department of Applied Sciences & Humanities provides foundational education in mathematics, physics, chemistry, and humanities.

Civil Engineering

The Department of Civil Engineering focuses on construction, infrastructure, structural engineering, and related engineering fields.

Electrical Engineering

The Department of Electrical Engineering focuses on electrical systems, power generation, circuits, and electrical technologies.

Electronics Engineering

The Department of Electronics Engineering focuses on electronic systems, communication, circuits, and related technologies.

Mechanical Engineering

The Department of Mechanical Engineering focuses on machines, manufacturing, mechanical systems, and industrial technologies.

Computer Science & Engineering

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering focuses on computer science, software development, information technology, and related computing fields.

Facilities

The institute provides various facilities to support the academic and practical needs of students.

Boys' Hostels

The institute provides separate hostel facilities for junior and senior male students, ensuring a comfortable and organized residential environment.

Girls' Hostel

The institute provides separate hostel facilities for female students with a safe, comfortable, and supportive residential environment.

Anti-Ragging Environment

The institute maintains a strict anti-ragging environment and promotes a safe, respectful, and friendly campus for all students.

Hostel Messes

Each hostel has a separate mess facility to provide students with convenient dining arrangements within their residential premises.

Canteens

The campus has two canteens that provide students and staff with convenient food and refreshment facilities.

Sports Grounds

The institute has two large sports grounds that provide students with facilities for outdoor sports and recreational activities.

Green Campus

Spread across approximately 150 acres, the campus has extensive green spaces and a pleasant environment that supports a healthy and peaceful academic atmosphere.

Workshop

The Workshop provides practical facilities for technical training and hands-on engineering activities.
