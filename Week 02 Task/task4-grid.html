About

Kamla Nehru Institute of Technology is a state government, autonomous engineering institution located in Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University. It has been ranked amongst the top engineering institutes under AKTU. College Founded: 1976.


Academic Programs

The institute offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes in various academic fields.

B.Tech

The B.Tech programme is an undergraduate programme with a duration of 4 years and 8 semesters.

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is a postgraduate programme with a duration of 2 years and 4 semesters.

M.Tech

The institute offers full-time and part-time postgraduate M.Tech programmes. The part-time programme consists of five semesters.
