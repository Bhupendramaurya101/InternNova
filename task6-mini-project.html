Welcome to World Explorer

World geography is the study of Earth's countries, continents, oceans, physical features, climates, environments, and people.

The Earth has a wide variety of landscapes, including mountains, deserts, forests, rivers, and oceans.

Learn more about Earth from NASA's Earth page .

Continents

Earth has seven continents. Each continent has different countries, cultures, environments, and geographical features.

Asia

Asia is the largest continent in the world by both area and population. India, China, Japan, and many other countries are located in Asia.

Europe

Europe is known for its rich history, diverse cultures, famous landmarks, architecture, and many languages.

Africa

Africa is known for its diverse landscapes, wildlife, cultures, and natural resources. The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world.

Famous Places

Eiffel Tower - France

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in Paris, France.

Taj Mahal - India

The Taj Mahal is a famous historical monument located in Agra, India.
