Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. It contains land, oceans, atmosphere, and many different ecosystems.
The chemical formula for water is H2O.
The area of a square can be written as A = s2.

World Geography

World geography is the study of Earth's countries, continents, oceans, physical features, climates, and people.

Continents

Europe

Europe is a continent known for its rich history, diverse cultures, famous landmarks, and many different languages.

France

France is a country in Western Europe. It is famous for its history, art, fashion, food, and architecture.

Paris

Paris is the capital of France. It is famous for landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre Museum.
Paris is also an important center of art, culture, and fashion.

Asia

Asia is the largest continent in the world by both area and population. It contains many countries with diverse cultures and environments.

India

India is a country in South Asia. It is known for its diverse cultures, languages, traditions, and history.

New Delhi

New Delhi is the capital of India and an important center of government, history, and culture.

Oceans

Pacific Ocean

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth.

Location

It lies between Asia and Australia to the west and North and South America to the east.

Importance

The Pacific Ocean plays an important role in Earth's climate, marine ecosystems, transportation, and global trade.
