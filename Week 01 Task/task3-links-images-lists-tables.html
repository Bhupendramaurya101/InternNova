Academic Programs

The institute offers the following courses under a semester system:

  1. Undergraduate programs: B.Tech
  2. Master of Computer Applications
  3. Full-time postgraduate (M.Tech)
  4. Part-time (M.Tech) programme (five semesters)
  5. Doctoral programmes
  6. Computer Science and Engineering
  7. Electronics Engineering
  8. Electrical Engineering
  9. Civil Engineering
  10. Applied Mathematics
  11. Applied Physics
  12. Applied Chemistry
  13. Humanities

Departments
