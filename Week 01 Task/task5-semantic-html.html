Continents

Earth has seven continents. Each continent has its own countries, cultures, climates, and geographical features.

Asia

Asia is the largest continent in the world by both area and population. It contains many countries, cultures, languages, and natural environments.

Europe

Europe is a continent known for its rich history, diverse cultures, famous landmarks, and many languages.

Africa

Africa is known for its diverse landscapes, wildlife, cultures, and natural resources.

Oceans

Oceans cover a large part of Earth's surface and play an important role in climate, marine life, and transportation.

Pacific Ocean

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth. It lies between Asia, Australia, and the Americas.

Atlantic Ocean

The Atlantic Ocean is the second-largest ocean. It separates the Americas from Europe and Africa.

Indian Ocean

The Indian Ocean is located mainly between Africa, Asia, Australia, and the Southern Ocean.

About Geography

Importance of Geography

Geography helps us understand Earth's physical features, people, places, climates, environments, and the relationship between humans and nature.
